Maple Street Market

Maple Street Market is an independent grocery store located at 418 Maple Street in the town of Cedar Falls. The store was founded in 1998 by Helen Park. In 2015, Helen retired and her nephew, Daniel Park, took over as the owner. The store employs 14 people. The day-to-day manager is Rosa Delgado, who has worked at the store since 2010.

The store is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, on Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and on Sunday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It is closed on two holidays each year: Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. The bakery counter stops serving at 4:00 PM every day, even though the rest of the store stays open later.

Maple Street Market has five departments: produce, bakery, deli, dairy, and frozen foods. In the produce section, regular apples cost $1.20 per pound, organic apples cost $2.40 per pound, and bananas cost $0.59 per pound. The bakery sells sourdough loaves for $4.50 each and blueberry muffins for $1.75 each. Sourdough loaves are delivered fresh to the store at 6:00 AM each morning. In the dairy section, a gallon of whole milk costs $3.25, a dozen large eggs costs $2.80, and sharp cheddar cheese costs $6.00 per pound. The deli sells sliced turkey for $8.50 per pound. In the grocery aisles, canned tomato soup costs $1.50 per can.

The store runs several promotions. Every Tuesday, all bakery items are 20% off. Canned soup is always "buy two, get one free." Customers who sign up for the free Maple Rewards card save 5% on every purchase; the Rewards discount is applied after all other discounts. Maple Street Market does not sell alcohol or tobacco. A small pharmacy counter operated inside the store from 2005 until it closed permanently in 2023.

Maple Street Market offers home delivery to addresses within 5 miles of the store. Delivery costs $4.00, but it is free for orders of $50.00 or more. The store has four regular checkout lanes and two self-checkout kiosks, and its parking lot has 30 spaces. Returns are accepted within 7 days of purchase with a receipt, except for perishable items, which cannot be returned. The weekly specials are posted on the store's website, and customers can contact the manager by email at manager@maplestreetmarket.example.
